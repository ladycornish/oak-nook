Self Catering Annex
By Galleywood Common

Gemma

"Spotlessly clean and cute annex with everything I needed for a short work visit. Parking is right outside which was handy. Bed, duvet and bed linen were very comfortable. Really clear instructions for using everything in the apartment. Morrisons Daily about a 5 min walk away which is very well stocked for a local corner shop. Didn’t have time to explore further unfortunately as I was there on business. But the bits I did see on the way to and from work looked beautiful! Thanks Mel for hosting me at such short notice!"

Tom

"Mel and Jon were fantastic hosts - so lovely and very accommodating. The place was absolutely spotless and we had a very comfortable nights sleep. Can’t recommend it highly enough!"
