Stock Road, Galleywood, Essex

Oak Nook is our self contained, one bedroom apartment which is situated on the edge of Galleywood Common, a local nature reserve in the Essex Countryside. Less than a minutes walk from your private front door you can access the ancient racetrack located within 125 acres of Common Land.

It features a cosy living room, a well equipped kitchen with a breakfast bar & stools, including Nespresso coffee machine, electric hob & double oven, full size dishwasher, microwave and fridge/freezer. Sky TV and an Alexa Bluetooth speaker are located in the living room for your use (Alexa unplugged). Wardrobe and further storage can be found in the lobby and linen and towels are provided for the bedroom & shower room.

With free offroad parking for one car, the driveway is covered by CCTV for your peace of mind.
