Self Catering Apartment
In The Essex Countryside

Stock Road, Galleywood, Essex

Oak Nook, your self-contained 36sq metre annex nestled on the edge of Galleywood Common, a stunning local nature reserve in the heart of picturesque Essex. Step out of your private front door and, in less than a minute, find yourself immersed in 125 acres of beautiful Galleywood Common. Explore ancient woodlands, stroll along the historic racetrack, or simply breathe in the fresh air and enjoy the abundant wildlife – a true haven for walkers and nature lovers.

Unwind in the spacious living room, where you can catch your favourite shows on the Smart TV or set the mood with your own music via the Bluetooth speaker – perfect for a relaxed evening in under dimmable lighting. The well-equipped kitchen invites you to dine in, featuring a stylish breakfast bar & stools, a Nespresso coffee machine & kettle for your morning brew, a modern induction hob & double oven, microwave, dishwasher, and a generous fridge/freezer – everything you need to feel at home.

In the bedroom, you’ll enjoy a sumptuous king-size bed enveloped in John Lewis premium bedding. Drift off to sleep behind blackout linings on our quality curtains aiding your sleep. Bedside power and touch lighting provide the perfect convenience for reading and charging. Beyond the bedroom, your carpeted lobby offers generous wardrobe space and additional storage. We provide crisp linen and fluffy towels for your comfort. The beautifully appointed shower room, with its soaking rainfall shower, truly defines the luxurious escape of Oak Nook.

For your peace of mind, free off-road parking for one car is available, with the driveway covered by CCTV.
